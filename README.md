**# School-Finance-Management-Dashboard**

Excel based school finance management tool for tracking bank transactions, receipts, payments, petty cash and analysing expenditure through dashboards.


**#Project Overview**

The School Finance Management and Reporting System is an Excel-based financial management tool developed to improve financial record-keeping, transaction tracking, expenditure monitoring, and financial reporting in a school environment.

The workbook brings together financial information from multiple bank accounts, receipts, payments, and petty cash records into a consolidated structure. It also provides a dashboard for analysing expenditure across different vote heads and months.

The project demonstrates how Microsoft Excel can be used to streamline financial operations, improve data organisation, and generate meaningful financial insights to support decision-making.

#**Key Features**

Multi-bank cash book management: Tracks financial transactions across three bank accounts.

Receipts tracking: Organises incoming payments and their corresponding details.

Expenditure tracking: Records payments by date, payee, purpose, amount and payment method.

Petty cash management: Maintains records of cash received and expenses incurred.

Transaction consolidation: Combines expenditure information from different sources.

Financial dashboard: Summarises expenditure by vote head and month.

Running balance calculations: Uses Excel formulas to calculate cash book balances.

**#Business Value**

The workbook provides a structured approach to managing school finances by bringing together records that would otherwise be maintained separately.

It supports better expenditure monitoring, facilitates the review of financial transactions, and makes it easier to identify spending patterns across different vote heads and reporting periods.
